Acquisition scope

HygieneRoll.com is available for strategic acquisition. This page states what exists, what can be inspected before an inquiry, and what is not claimed.

No price, valuation, buyer interest, or deal terms are published here.

Strategic scope

HygieneRoll is built around one moment: when household hygiene consumption turns back into replenishment demand. Paper hygiene is the entry point, and the same logic extends to personal care and household hygiene essentials.

The asset is a category-native name with a working public layer. A visitor can run a replenishment calculator, inspect the method behind every number, and read a governed vocabulary that keeps definitions, calculations, assumptions, and external evidence apart.

What can be evaluated

The domain and the developed work are described separately. This page describes what exists and can be inspected. It does not state that any component will be included in a transaction. Scope, and any rights that pass, would be set only in a written agreement between the parties. Nothing on this page is an offer or a statement of terms.

The domain

The name hygieneroll.com, which currently serves this site.

Replenishment instrument

A deterministic, browser-only engine that computes depletion windows, planning triggers, reorder thresholds, fixed-interval fit, and lead-time slip exposure from user assumptions, with an automated test suite.

What is not claimed

  • No operating company, customers, subscribers, revenue, or traction.
  • No inventory, fulfillment operation, supplier agreements, or live subscription product.
  • No proprietary consumption data. The calculator contains no embedded benchmarks.
  • HygieneRoll does not represent that any trademark registration exists for the name. This describes what this page claims. It is not a legal opinion on trademark status.
  • No ownership or transferability of third-party rights, such as licensed fonts, software, or other materials, is represented.
  • No market-size figure is published. The site shows a third-party estimate only after its source is admitted under the evidence policy.
  • Interface screens are concepts, not a deployed product.
