# HygieneRoll Public Decision Log

This log records public reference decisions only. It does not record pricing, valuation, buyer selection, negotiation, outreach, or private commercial plans.

## DEC-HR-001

Date: 2026-09-24

Reference claims use four explicit classes: `PROJECT_DEFINITION`, `OBSERVED_EVIDENCE`, `DERIVED_INTERPRETATION`, and `ILLUSTRATIVE_MODEL`.

## DEC-HR-002

Date: 2026-09-24

Project definitions are not treated as external evidence. HygieneRoll pages and the calculator methodology may show where a concept comes from. They do not establish facts about markets, households, or companies.

## DEC-HR-003

Date: 2026-09-24

Observed external claims fail closed without admissible evidence. A missing source blocks the claim. The sentence is not softened while the unsupported fact remains.

## DEC-HR-004

Date: 2026-09-24

Phase II begins with one reference hub, `reference.html`, not with a separate page for each concept.

## DEC-HR-005

Date: 2026-09-24

The first eight knowledge objects are project definitions unless external evidence is separately admitted. Foundation v0.1 admits no external sources.

## DEC-HR-006

Date: 2026-09-27

Any external factual claim published on any public HygieneRoll surface is subject to the same Evidence Admission Law, not only claims inside the reference layer. That includes the homepage, the replenishment model, the calculator, explanatory copy, and any future page. A new public page is governed from the moment it exists.

## DEC-HR-007

Date: 2026-09-27

Source admission and claim admission are separate gates. An `ADMITTED` source is trustworthy in general. It is not evidence for every claim. An external claim is admissible only when each cited source is admitted and has a claim-source qualification for that exact claim in which scope, period, geography, population, metric, and wording all pass. A false or missing dimension blocks the claim. Absence of qualification is not permission.

## DEC-HR-008

Date: 2026-09-27

The source object is a fail-closed node. It carries `id`, `title`, `publisher`, `url`, `source_type`, `publication_date`, `accessed_date`, `geography`, `population`, `scope`, `metric`, `rights_note`, `admission_status`, and `notes`. A source with any material omission cannot be `ADMITTED`, whatever status it declares.

## DEC-HR-009

Date: 2026-09-27

The two external claims published before DEC-HR-006, the subscription economy figures and the broad cleaning and hygiene products market figures, are registered as legacy external claims pending governance migration. They are not grandfathered: a pending legacy claim is never admissible, and each must be admitted through the v0.2 contract or suppressed by governance v0.3. The source register stays empty until the engine and its tests are complete.
