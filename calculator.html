Household Hygiene Replenishment Calculator

Estimate when recurring household hygiene supplies enter their replenishment window — using your own usage, lead-time, and buffer assumptions.

No hidden consumption benchmarks. No account required.

Scenario

v0.1 supports up to 10 items per scenario.

Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not stored by this tool.

How this estimate works

Every number comes from what you enter. The calculator does not contain an average household, a hidden usage table, or a claim about how fast a product is actually used.

Current supply is divided by the usage you observed or estimated. That produces a weekly rate, whether you entered units per week or how long the current quantity usually lasts. Lead time and your safety buffer are then subtracted from that depletion timing. If you set a variability allowance, the window widens on both sides. At 0%, it stays a single estimate.

The result is a planning estimate. Actual consumption may differ. A subscription asks when something should ship again. This tool asks when the household is likely to need the item again, and it answers only from the assumptions you supply.

Formulas

Weekly method: weekly_usage = units per week.

Duration method: weekly_usage = current_quantity / duration_weeks.

Variability v is the entered percent divided by 100, from 0 to 0.50. Slow usage is weekly_usage × (1 − v). Fast usage is weekly_usage × (1 + v).

Depletion days are (quantity / usage) × 7. At 0% variability the day is rounded to the nearest whole day. Above 0%, the early bound is floored and the late bound is ceiled.

Planning trigger days are depletion − lead time − buffer, then clamped at 0. Illustrative cadence uses the same day rule with the replenishment quantity, or the current quantity if that field is blank.

Limitations

  • Estimates depend on the inputs you provide.
  • Household behavior can change.
  • Product sizes and unit definitions vary.
  • The tool does not predict actual consumption.
  • The variability allowance is user-selected, not a statistical confidence interval.
  • The output is a planning aid, not medical or sanitation-safety guidance.
  • v0.1 contains no embedded consumption benchmarks.

Related

The reference for this loop is the replenishment model. Different essentials can deplete on different clocks. This calculator makes those clocks legible. It does not turn them into one subscription.
